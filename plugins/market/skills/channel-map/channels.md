# Channel catalog

24 distribution channels in 4 funnel stages, from Shann Holmberg's "How to Build Distribution When Anyone Can Clone Your Product" (2 Nov 2026).

Modes, how you get the channel: **B** build it, **P** partner with someone who has it, **R** rent it, **A** acquire it.

## 1. Before they know they need you

People have the problem and aren't looking for a product yet.

| # | Channel | Modes | Reaches buyers by | Check a competitor | Check the category |
| :- | :- | :- | :- | :- | :- |
| 01 | Founder & team audiences | B P | Useful posts and conversations on X, LinkedIn, TikTok, Instagram; followers become customers, partners and referrals | Founders' and team's followers, posts in the last 30 days | People the buyer follows on the topic |
| 02 | YouTube channels | B R A | Tutorials, demos, comparisons and interviews for people who know the category | Own channel: subscribers, last upload. Sponsored segments in category videos | Top channels for the buyer questions, subscribers |
| 03 | Newsletters & email lists | B R A | Subscribers who chose to hear from you | Signup form on site; sponsorships in category newsletters | Newsletters the buyer reads, subscribers, public sponsorship price |
| 04 | Creators & industry experts | P R | Posts by people the buyer already follows; clipping networks paid by views | "<competitor> review" on YouTube, TikTok, X; paid-partnership labels | Creators covering the category, audience size |
| 05 | Press, podcasts & industry publications | B P R A | Coverage, which also feeds AI answers: most links AI tools cite come from earned media | News search; "<founder> podcast" | Publications and podcasts the buyer follows |
| 06 | Events, webinars & workshops | B P R | Gathering people around the problem; speaking at or sponsoring others' events | Events or webinars pages; sponsor lists of category conferences | Conferences, meetups and hackathons for the buyer |
| 07 | Free tools, templates & open source | B A | Meeting the problem before a product search: calculator, template, dataset, plugin, open-source tool | Free tools on site; GitHub repos and stars | Popular free tools for the job, users or stars |
| 08 | Social, video & offline ads | R | Paid reach beyond your audience | Meta Ad Library, LinkedIn company page Ads tab, TikTok Creative Center: active ads | — |
| 09 | Direct outreach & sales | B | Researched emails, calls and messages to the right people at the right companies | "Book a demo" or "Talk to sales"; sales job posts | — |
| 10 | Education & certification | B P R | Teaching the skill the product helps with: courses, certificates, student and campus programs | Academy or certification pages | Courses and bootcamps teaching the skill |

## 2. While they compare options

People know they need a product and look at the options.

| # | Channel | Modes | Reaches buyers by | Check a competitor | Check the category |
| :- | :- | :- | :- | :- | :- |
| 11 | Your website & search visibility | B A | Pages answering what buyers ask before buying: comparisons, use cases, pricing, tutorials. Ranking in search doesn't mean being cited in AI answers | Comparison and alternatives pages; rank for the buyer questions | Who ranks, and who AI answers cite, for the buyer questions |
| 12 | Review & comparison sites | B R | Proof from other customers: G2, Capterra, Trustpilot, app-store reviews | Review count and rating per site | The category's page on each site, products listed |
| 13 | Marketplaces & app stores | B | Buying through an account they already pay for: app stores, plugin directories, browser extensions, cloud marketplaces | Listings, installs, reviews | Marketplaces the buyer buys from |
| 14 | Launch & discovery platforms | B R A | Visitors around a launch: Product Hunt, Hacker News, niche directories, launch newsletters | Product Hunt launches and rank; Show HN posts | Directories and launch lists for the category |
| 15 | Search & AI assistant ads | R | Ads at the moment of search, including inside AI assistants | Google Ads Transparency Center; sponsored results on the buyer questions | Whether the buyer questions show ads at all |

## 3. Through tools and people they trust

People take recommendations from the tools, advisors and groups they already use.

| # | Channel | Modes | Reaches buyers by | Check a competitor | Check the category |
| :- | :- | :- | :- | :- | :- |
| 16 | Integrations & technology partners | B P | Partner directories, joint campaigns, recommendations during a partner's onboarding | Integrations page; listings in partners' directories | Tools the buyer already pays for that have app directories |
| 17 | AI agent surfaces | B P | MCP servers, connectors and apps inside customers' AI workflows, listed in agent directories | MCP server, ChatGPT or Claude app, registry listings | Agent directories for the category |
| 18 | Agencies, consultants & resellers | P | Businesses that already advise the buyer recommend, implement or sell the product | Partner program page, partner directory size | Agencies and consultants serving the buyer |
| 19 | Affiliates & referral partners | P | People paid per customer to recommend | Affiliate program and payout; PartnerStack or Impact listing | Affiliates already promoting the category |
| 20 | Communities | B P A | Customers helping each other: Discord, Slack, forums, memberships | Own community, members, activity in the last 30 days | Subreddits, Discords, Slack groups and forums the buyer uses, members |
| 21 | Businesses with an existing customer base | A | Buying a business whose customers could use the product | Acquisitions in news or Crunchbase | Small businesses serving the buyer |
| 22 | Bundles, pre-installs & default placement | P R | A partner includes the product in something it already sells. The customer still has to choose it | "Included with" pages; telco, hardware or suite deals | Products the buyer gets bundled |

## 4. After they buy

The product and its customers reach the next customers.

| :- | :- | :- | :- | :- | :- |
| 23 | Distribution built into the product | B | Shared documents, public pages, invites, "Made with" badges. Forcing an invite into every action usually makes the product worse | Free signup: badges, public pages, invite prompts; search "made with <competitor>" | — |
| 24 | Customer referrals & word of mouth | B | Asking after a successful outcome, with a reward for both sides. Check that referred customers become valuable before raising payouts | Referral program page | — |
